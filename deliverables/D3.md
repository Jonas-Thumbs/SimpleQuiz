# D.3 Analysis

## 1. System Description
Teachers need an efficient way to create, organize and manage quizzes while students need a secure and accessible way to take quizzes and see their results. SimpleQuiz is an online quiz platform that is designed for teachers and students who want a simple alternative to more complicated quiz applications in learning management systems. Its value proposition is to improve quiz creation and management for teachers while allowing students to complete quizzes, save their progress, and receive feedback to support their learning.
