## 3. Interviews summary
Liya Kass (Worker and Student at NAU)

    How often do you take quizzes for your classes?
    * About 1 to 2 a week per class

    Where do you normally take quizzes?
    * Online (canvas)
    * On Paper

    Are there any features on your quizzes that differ from normal quizzes?
    * Unlimited Attempts
    * Build as you go
    * Cant retract

    How do you feel when you can't go back to a previous question?
    * Nervous to get it right the first time
    * Feel like time runs faster
        
    Before starting a new attempt, do you study or just memorize the answers?
    * Depends on the quiz and how hard it is
    * If short on time, memorize the answers
    * Interesting subject, then study
    * Elective classes, memorize answers

    How do you feel about quizzes that change questions after each attempt?
    * Feels like a whole new quiz
    * Takes more time
